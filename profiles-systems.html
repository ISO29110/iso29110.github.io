Systems engineering profiles

For VSEs that develop systems: products made of hardware and software elements, such as devices, equipment or embedded products. The systems engineering profiles follow the same four-level road map as the software profiles, and take their processes from ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288. They are for non-critical systems.

LEVEL 1

Entry profile

ISO/IEC 29110-5-6-1 is a stand-alone management and engineering guide for start-up VSEs and small system projects. It covers the Project Management and System Definition and Realization processes with a lighter set of tasks than Basic.

AGREED SCOPE

Agreed requirements and deliverables

Project requirements and deliverables are agreed with the acquirer before work starts.

VISIBILITY

Disciplined project management

The project is visible, so problems and deviations are spotted and corrected early.

QUALITY

A systematic realization process

The system is defined and built in a way that meets the acquirer's needs with a quality product.

LEVEL 2

Basic profile

ISO/IEC 29110-5-6-2 is for VSEs that develop a single product with a single work team. It gives a stand-alone guide to two processes. The acquirer's Product Statement of Work starts the project. VSEs whose software is part of a larger system, or who build stand-alone software, are encouraged to use the software Basic guide (29110-5-1-2) as well.

LEVEL 3

Intermediate profile

ISO/IEC 29110-5-6-3 is for VSEs running several projects in parallel with several work teams. It extends the Basic profile's processes with more objectives, tasks and work products, and adds organisation-level processes.

BM · NEW

Business Management

A new process for managing the VSE as a business across its projects and teams.

PM

Project Management

Extended from Basic for multiple projects running at the same time.

SR

System Definition and Realization

Extended from Basic with additional tasks and work products.

AM · CONDITIONAL

Acquisition Management

Needed only when a product or service must be obtained from an external supplier.

New roles include Business Manager, Acquisition Manager and Proposal Manager. The current edition (TR 29110-5-6-3:2019) maps its objectives to ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288 and ISO 9001. VSEs that provide services after delivery can also use the service delivery guidelines (29110-5-3).

LEVEL 4

Advanced profile

ISO/IEC 29110-5-6-4:2025 is for VSEs that want to sustain and grow as independent system development organisations. It builds on the Intermediate profile, adding or changing processes, tasks, work products and roles for multi-project, multi-team work. It also opens with a clause on systems thinking.

TM

Technical Management

Technical management across the VSE's projects.

PM

Project Management

Planning, executing, controlling and closing projects.

SR

System Definition and Realization

Requirements, architecture, construction, integration, verification, validation and delivery.

AM

Acquisition Management

Obtaining products or services from external suppliers.

SYTD

System Transition and Disposal

Moving the system into use and, at the end of its life, retiring it.

Before starting a project, the guide expects documented needs, a completed feasibility study, an assigned team with a project manager and systems engineer, and available resources. Annex A lists systems engineering Deployment Packages.
