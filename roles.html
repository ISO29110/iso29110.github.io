08 · Roles

You don't need to hire seven people

The standard defines responsibilities, not headcount. In a small company one person typically covers several roles. The only rule of thumb is that work should be checked by someone other than its author. The 2025 Basic guide also describes a Support Manager role for the optional support process (Annex A).
