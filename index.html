For owners & managers of small software businesses

Win customer trust with a standard built for small companies.

ISO/IEC 29110 is a family of international standards and guides for Very Small Entities: companies, departments or project teams of up to 25 people. It covers software and systems engineering, organisational management, service delivery, agile and DevOps, and domain-specific profiles such as space software. It gives you a proven, lightweight way to deliver on time, reduce rework, and show clients that your process meets an international benchmark, without the cost and paperwork of standards designed for large enterprises.
