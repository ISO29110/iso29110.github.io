Software Engineering Standards · Study Guide

Good engineering practice, sized for very small teams.

ISO/IEC 29110 defines lifecycle profiles for Very Small Entities (VSEs): companies, departments or projects with up to 25 people. It gives small teams a lightweight, auditable way to plan projects and build software without the overhead of standards written for large organisations.

01 · Why it exists

The problem 29110 solves

Most software is built by small organisations, yet standards such as ISO/IEC/IEEE 12207 and models like CMMI are hard for a five-person team to adopt. ISO/IEC 29110 extracts a coherent subset of those practices and packages it with free, practical guides.

VSE

Who it's for

Start-ups, small software houses, internal IT teams and individual projects of up to 25 people.

PROFILE

What it contains

A "profile" is a selected set of processes, activities, tasks, roles and work products drawn from larger base standards.

GUIDE

How it's used

Management & Engineering Guides describe exactly what to do, who does it, and what each activity produces.

CERT

How it's verified

VSEs can be assessed or certified against a profile, which signals process maturity to customers.

03 · Document set

How the standard is organised

ISO/IEC 29110 is a family of documents. Parts 1, 2 and 4 are aimed at standards producers and assessors; Part 5 guides are written for the VSE itself.

PartTitle (short)Main audience
29110-1Overview: concepts, terms and the VSE contextEveryone
29110-2Framework and taxonomy for creating profilesStandards producers
29110-3Assessment, certification and audit guidanceAssessors, auditors, VSEs
29110-4Profile specifications (normative requirements)Standards producers, tool vendors
29110-5Management & Engineering Guides for each profileVSEs and students

Parallel guides also exist for systems engineering (systems that include hardware as well as software) and for agile and DevOps contexts.

04 · The Basic Profile

Two processes that work together

The Basic profile is the most widely taught. Project Management (PM) plans and controls the work; Software Implementation (SI) builds the product. The Statement of Work from the customer kicks everything off.

Statement of Work→ PM.1 Planning→ PM.2 Execution ⇄ PM.3 Control⇄ SI.1 – SI.6→ PM.4 Closure→ Delivered product
05 · Roles

Who does what

In a VSE one person often holds several roles. The standard defines the responsibilities, not the job titles.
