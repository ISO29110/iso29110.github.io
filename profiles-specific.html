Specific profiles: space software

The generic profiles serve VSEs in any domain that do not develop critical software. Specific profiles are for VSEs in a particular domain with its own requirements. The first specific profiles are for the space domain. ISO/IEC 29110-7-1:2026 gives guidelines for implementing them through a set of development processes.

In the ISO/IEC 29110 numbering, Part 6 holds specific profile specifications and Part 7 holds the guidelines for implementing them.

Who the space guidelines are for

AUDIENCE

Small space-software organisations

Small suppliers, developers, managers and quality assurance managers of software products for the space domain.

SELF-IMPOSED

Usable by a single party

One organisation can also use the guidelines on its own, by applying a self-imposed set of processes.

ANY LIFE CYCLE

No prescribed method

The guidelines do not prescribe a specific system or software life cycle model, development methodology, model or technique.

Three space profiles, in increasing maturity

The draft specification (29110-6-1) defines three space profiles as incremental maturity levels. They add space requirements to the software Basic profile (29110-4-1) and the Organisational Management profile (29110-4-2), and apply to software in the lowest criticality categories, C and D, of the European space standard ECSS-Q-ST-80C.

Level 1

  1. PMProject Management
  2. SISoftware Implementation
  3. STSoftware Testing
  4. QAQuality Assurance
  5. CMConfiguration Management
  6. PRProblem Resolution
  7. RMResource Management
  8. PPMProject Portfolio Management

Level 2 adds

  1. RERequirements Elicitation
  2. VEVerification
  3. SDASafety and Dependability Assurance

Level 3 adds

  1. OMOrganisational Management
  2. PSMProcess Management

Profile content as described in the 2021 draft specification. Check the published 2026 guidelines for the final details.
