Organisational management

Organisational management profile

Once a VSE runs projects or services well, the next step is to manage the organisation as a whole. ISO/IEC 29110-5-2-1:2025 gives management guidelines for the organisational profile specified in ISO/IEC 29110-4-2. It applies to VSEs that develop software or run operational processes, and it can be used with any processes, techniques or methods that help a VSE improve customer satisfaction and productivity.

Four management processes

OM

Organisational Management

Runs the VSE in line with its business strategy and keeps track of customer satisfaction.

PPM

Product Portfolio Management

Manages the set of products and projects the VSE takes on, so that the work running in parallel supports the strategy.

RM

Resource Management

Controls how people, infrastructure and other resources are provided to projects and services.

PSM

Process Management

Defines standard processes, deploys them across projects and improves them over time.

Is your VSE ready?

The guide is meant for VSEs that:

  • Already run at least one operational process, carried out through projects or services, such as those in the Basic profiles or the service delivery guidelines
  • Know their business and want to formalise how it is managed
  • Have identified internal or external sources of good practice to base their processes on
HOW IT FITS

On top of your operational profile

The organisational profile does not replace the software, systems or service delivery profiles. It adds management of the whole VSE above them. Like the rest of the series, it works with waterfall, iterative, incremental, evolutionary or agile life cycles.

What changed in the 2025 edition

STATUS

An International Standard

The first edition as an International Standard replaces the 2016 Technical Report.

WIDER USE

Any VSE with an operational process

It now applies to any VSE with at least one operational process implemented by projects or services, not only to VSEs using a Basic profile.

SIMPLER

Aligned and streamlined

The text was edited to align with the other parts of the series. The former Annexes A and B were removed, and the mapping to base standards now lives in ISO/IEC 29110-4-2.
