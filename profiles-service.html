Service delivery

Service delivery profile

ISO/IEC 29110-5-3 guides VSEs that deliver services to customers, such as operating, supporting and maintaining software and systems after development and installation. A VSE can use it as an internal service provider or as an external, commercial provider. It can be used on its own, or alongside the software and systems engineering guides.

Four service delivery processes

GO

Governance

Sets the direction and objectives for the services and oversees how they are delivered and improved.

CO

Service Control

Keeps services stable by controlling changes, with a Control Manager who approves or rejects them.

RE

Service Relationship

Manages the interfaces with customers and suppliers and the service catalogue, led by a Relationship Manager.

IN

Service Incident

Handles incidents and restores service, with an Incident Manager who has authority over all incidents.

How it works

FLEXIBLE ORDER

Tasks in the order that suits you

Tasks are linked by their inputs and outputs. Their numbering only identifies them, so a VSE can sequence the work to fit its own services.

TAILORED

No single uniform approach

Each VSE sets its own service objectives and improvement initiatives to match its customers and resources.

ISO/IEC 20000

A step towards service management

The profile draws on ISO/IEC 20000, the service management system standard, and its specification maps to ISO/IEC 20000-1.
